Classic Lasagna Recipe

Description

This classic lasagna recipe is the ultimate comfort food. Layers of tender pasta, rich and savory meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted mozzarella come together to create a dish that's both hearty and satisfying. Perfect for family dinners or special occasions, this lasagna is sure to impress everyone at the table.

Whether you're cooking for a crowd or preparing a cozy meal at home, this homemade lasagna is a timeless favorite. Serve it with garlic bread and a fresh salad for a complete and delicious meal!

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 3 cups mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  3. In a skillet, cook ground beef until browned. Drain excess fat.
  4. Layer sauce, noodles, ricotta, beef, and mozzarella in a baking dish.
  5. Repeat layers and top with Parmesan cheese.
  6. Bake for 25–30 minutes until bubbly and golden.
  7. Let it cool for 10 minutes before serving. Enjoy!
