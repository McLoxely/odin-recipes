Fresh Garden Salad Recipe

Description

This fresh garden salad is a crisp and refreshing side dish, packed with fresh veggies and tossed with a light vinaigrette. Perfect for any meal!

Simple, colorful, and full of flavor, this salad is an easy way to add some greens to your plate.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed greens
  • 1/2 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine greens, cucumber, tomatoes, and red onion.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk olive oil and balsamic vinegar. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Drizzle dressing over the salad and toss gently.
  4. Serve immediately for a fresh, crisp salad.
